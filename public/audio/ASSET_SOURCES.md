# Ashvault audio asset sources

The ambience tracks in this folder were downloaded from OpenGameArt entries released as **CC0 / public domain**. They may be used without attribution.

- `dungeon002_0.ogg` — https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-ambience
- `dark_cavern_ambient_001.ogg`, `dark_cavern_ambient_002.ogg` — https://opengameart.org/content/dark-cavern-ambient
- `cold_silence.ogg` — https://opengameart.org/content/cold-silence
- `bleeding_out2_2.ogg` — https://opengameart.org/content/bleeding-out
- `blackout_3mzut0qtwao.mp3` — https://opengameart.org/content/blackout
- `cathedral_in_the_forest.ogg` — https://opengameart.org/content/cathedral-in-the-forest-ambient-loop
- `somnium.mp3` — https://opengameart.org/content/somnium
- `boss_fight_lisboa.mp3` — https://opengameart.org/content/boss-fight-0

The active soundtrack uses the last three tracks: a dark bell-and-pad loop, a mournful theme, and a composed boss piece. Short interface cues remain deliberately small synthesized effects.
