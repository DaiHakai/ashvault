What this adds up to

Mostly agrees

Two thirds of the doc is either already built or a free addition. The dice system, initiative, HP curve, bank rules and permadeath economics all match — often argued from the same reasoning. This is not a rewrite of the foundations.

Six need a party

The Downed window, contested revival, the Zombie fork, corpse looting and the Rift Room all require other players in the room. Solo, a ten-minute revival timer nobody can answer is strictly worse than instant death — ten minutes of waiting to be told you already lost.

Three real forks

Classes vs weapon × background. Four of the five classes already exist as weapons; only the Necromancer is new. Bonus stacking. Turning stacking off would flatten 32 distinct kits back toward 9. Trinkets. Dropping the slot orphans all three rare boss drops.

Eight would vanish

The doc is silent on advantage, the shrine rest, the First Camp, the ten prologues, boss rooms you cannot flee, and the memorial. Adopting it as a clean replacement would delete these without ever saying so — which is the real risk of treating a from-scratch bible as a patch.

Best single idea

The Necromancer and the Zombie fork. It turns death from a full stop into a branch, and the Warden's Devour mirroring the Zombie's own ability ties the boss to the game's spine instead of being a one-off gimmick. Worth building even if nothing else here lands.
